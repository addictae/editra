Editra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Information stored

Editra may store Discord user and server IDs, leveling information, Coins, activity statistics, profile/social links, and bot configuration needed to provide its features.

How information is used

This information is used to operate profiles, leveling, rewards, leaderboards, notifications, and other Editra features.

Sharing

Editra does not intentionally sell user information. Information may be processed by Discord and hosting/infrastructure providers as necessary to operate the bot.

Data removal

For requests to remove stored Editra data, contact the Editra server owner and provide the Discord account or server involved.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Editra's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Editra server owner.
